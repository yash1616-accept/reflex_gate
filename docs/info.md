1. The Market Problem
Companies deploying autonomous AI agents (browser agents, financial bots, customer support swarms, coding agents) face a major bottleneck: Every agent action requires safety verification, tool routing, and policy checking.

If you use traditional generative LLMs (like GPT-4o or Claude) to inspect every action step:

Latency is unusable: Adds 1.5 to 5 seconds of delay per step.

Costs explode: A 10-step agent workflow costs $0.30–$0.90 just for inspection.

Hallucinated formatting: Parsing errors break automated state loops.
